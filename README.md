# PulseGuard: First 10 Minutes

An interactive website that trains ordinary people to respond to heart emergencies before the ambulance arrives. Built from **Module 2: The Cardiovascular System**.

## The problem

Heart disease is the world's leading cause of death. Many people who collapse could survive, but bystanders freeze, mistake a heart attack for "gas", or don't know CPR. Without CPR, the chance of surviving a cardiac arrest drops by about 7–10% every minute.

## Features

- **Live scenarios.** Four choose-your-action stories: a collapse at the gym (cardiac arrest), a grandfather's chest pain (heart attack), a mother's "acidity" (silent symptoms), and a 12-hour desk job (prevention). Each choice moves a clock and a survival or heart-health meter, and comes with feedback.
- **CPR rhythm trainer.** Tap the chest or press the space bar to practise 100–120 compressions a minute, with a live gauge and a metronome.
- **Heart lab.** A simple "house with 4 rooms" explainer with animated blood flow, step cards, and a heartbeat button.
- **Heart-health check.** Sliders and toggles that give an educational habit score and personal tips.
- **Pulse, the AI coach.** A chat assistant that explains choices, debriefs scenarios and builds plans. It falls back to built-in answers when AI isn't available.
- **Emergency band** with India's numbers: 112 (all emergencies) and 108 (ambulance).

## Run it

It's a single self-contained file. Open `index.html` in any browser, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repo.

The AI chat uses Claude's in-artifact `sample` capability. Outside Claude it automatically uses its offline answers.

> Educational tool only. It does not replace a certified CPR course or medical advice. In an emergency, call 112.
